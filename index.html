Welcome to North Star Bakery

We are a small neighborhood bakery two blocks from the Bell Street bus stop. Every loaf is mixed, shaped, and baked by hand in the shop you walk into, and the case is stocked fresh each morning for commuters, families, and anyone who needs a cake for Saturday.

What We Are Known For

Neighbors come back for three things, and we bake all of them every day we are open.

  • Slow-fermented sourdough rested a full 24 hours before it ever sees the oven.
  • Butter pastries laminated by hand each morning, out of the oven by 7:00 a.m.
  • Celebration cakes decorated to order for birthdays, showers, and block parties.
