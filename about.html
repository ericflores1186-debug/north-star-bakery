About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery opened in 1998 in a former hardware store, with one deck oven and a hand-lettered sign. Bea Alvarez started baking four kinds of bread for the neighbors on her block, and word traveled down Bell Street from there.

Twenty-eight years later we still bake in the same room, and a good share of our morning regulars are the children of our first customers. We stay small on purpose: everything sold here is made here, by people who live within a few miles of the shop.

How We Source

We buy as close to home as the season allows, and we would rather run out of something than substitute an ingredient we cannot stand behind.

  • Flour: stone-milled wheat from a family mill 60 miles west of the shop, delivered weekly.
  • Butter and cream: from a Minnesota dairy cooperative, never frozen.
  • Fruit: bought at the Wednesday growers' market from June through October, which is why hand pie flavors change.
  • Day-old bread: donated each evening to the Bell Street community pantry.
